WHY WE’RE HERE

For the moments that become part of you.

Zarat began with a simple belief: fragrance should be part of everyday life. A familiar note can bring back a place, a person, or a version of yourself you want to remember.

That is why we bring together scents for different moods and moments—soft or bold, bright or deep. Choose one for the day ahead, or the memory you want to leave behind.
